Quality Improvement (QI)

Quality Improvement (QI) is about working together to make our services better for everyone, one small step at a time. Quality Improvement is not just a way of doing things it's about making our work better, safer, and easier so that the people who use our services have an improved experience

Not all learning happens in a classroom.

Learning half day's are a short block of time where teams stop their normal work so they can learn, think, and plan small changes together.

Continuous Quality Improvement Trust-wide Framework

Model for Improvement

Quality Improvement (QI) is a structured approach to making continuous, positive changes across health and care services. Teams can use their Learning Half Day time to test ideas, solve everyday challenges, and measure impact.

The 3 Fundamental QI Questions:
  • 1. Aim: What are we trying to accomplish?
  • 2. Measures: How will we know that a change is an improvement?
  • 3. Ideas: What changes can we make that will result in improvement?
The PDSA Cycle
  • Plan: Identify the change and plan data collection.
  • Do: Carry out the small-scale test and record findings.
  • Study: Analyze results against your predicted aim.
  • Act: Decide whether to adopt, adapt, or abandon the change.
What Improvement Actually Is
  • Everyone has ideas and can make meaningful improvements.
  • Start small, test ideas out, and do what is manageable.
  • An opportunity to learn, experiment, and share learning across teams.
